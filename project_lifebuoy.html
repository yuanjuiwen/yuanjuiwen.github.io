Lifebuoy

Reimagining how a drowning person enters the lifebuoy, enabling them to receive rescue support with less effort and in a more instinctive posture, while maintaining stability and safety even in chaotic conditions.

2024/02

Role User research, Interaction design, Prototyping
Team 1 Computer Science student, 2 Industrial Design students

context

When Standard Rescue Gear Meets Panic and Moving Water

Conventional ring-style lifebuoys often assume calm conditions and deliberate, two-handed placement. In open water, waves and fear can make it hard to align the body with a small opening—delaying support when seconds matter. This project asks how entry could follow a more instinctive, low-effort motion while keeping the swimmer stable once they are in.
