"What if we could intuitively feel that we're too close to the screen without any notification at all?"

2024/02

Role User research, Interaction design, Prototyping
Team 1 Computer Science student, 2 Industrial Design students

A screen eye-care tool inspired by mydriatic drops. Mydriatics naturally blur near-distance vision — we apply the same principle to screens. Using the laptop's front camera to detect viewing distance, the screen produces corresponding levels of blur, replacing intrusive alerts and naturally guiding users to correct their posture without disrupting workflow.
